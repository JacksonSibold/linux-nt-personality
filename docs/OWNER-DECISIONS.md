# Owner decisions

Everything the review and the audit need the project owner to decide, in one list. Derived on 2026-10-12 from:

- the `Blocked` and "needs owner" statuses in [docs/review/](review/INDEX.md) (41 findings are `Blocked`, and some `Partly` findings have an owner question in the same status line);
- the open items in the resolution sections of [docs/review/audit-fixes.md](review/audit-fixes.md) (passes 2 to 4);
- the decision records that are `Proposed` or `Blocked` in [docs/decisions/INDEX.md](decisions/INDEX.md) (23 records).

Priority:

- **P1**: a reachable security or data-integrity defect, or a High finding. Decide first.
- **P2**: a correctness, build or contract gap that blocks other work.
- **P3**: a design, policy or cleanup choice with a Low or Medium finding.

"Recommended option" is the option the record marks as recommended, or the option the audit or review suggests. Where a record gives no recommendation, the row says so. "Consequence of deferring" is what stays true while the question is open.

## Notes

- Some items above are the same defect reported in two trees (for example arch M-2 and kernel-nt M-1, and arch IO-3 and kernel-nt IO-2). Decide them together.
- OD-14 is not a decision about code: it corrects a status. It is listed so the arch report is updated when the owner confirms record 0027.
- Each open decision record has a `Status` line. When a decision is made, update that record, its line in [docs/decisions/INDEX.md](decisions/INDEX.md), and the matching review finding.
